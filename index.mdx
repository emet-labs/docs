Emet Labs builds tools that make the behavior of AI agents verifiable. This site documents two tools.

Sentinel is our verification platform. trace-grab is the tool we use to collect trace data for that work. trace-grab never shows us sensitive data.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sentinel" icon="shield-check" href="/sentinel">
    Runtime verification for AI agents. Sentinel checks what a deployment did against Specifications that you write. It gives you an audit-ready answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="trace-grab" icon="terminal" href="/trace-grab">
    trace-grab is not a product. You use it to donate trace data to our research. It sanitizes and tokenizes all data on your machine. You read and approve the output before you send anything.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
