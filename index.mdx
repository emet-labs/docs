Emet Labs builds tools for making AI agent behavior verifiable. This site covers Sentinel, our
verification platform, and trace-grab, the tool we use to collect trace data for that work
without ever seeing anything sensitive.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sentinel" icon="shield-check" href="/sentinel">
    Runtime verification for AI agents: check what a deployment actually did against rules you
    define, and get an audit-ready answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="trace-grab" icon="terminal" href="/trace-grab">
    Not a product — a way to donate trace data to our research. Sanitizes and tokenizes
    everything locally; you read and approve the output before anything is sent.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
