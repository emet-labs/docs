---
title: "Raw event retention"
description: "Configure how long Sentinel retains raw events per tenant, and how the retention horizon is stamped on every verdict's assurance envelope."
---

Raw events are the evidence verdicts are built from, and they do not live forever. A
retention policy bounds how long the store keeps them: a reaper drops evidence past the
horizon, appends outside the admission window are refused, and every sealed verdict records
the horizon it was evaluated under on its assurance envelope. A verdict therefore stays
auditable after its underlying events age out.

<Note>
Retention ships as a library surface today: the policy lives on the store's
`ControlPlaneStore` trait, reaping on `RetentionStore`, and the envelope stamp on the core
`Evaluator`. No HTTP endpoint sets the policy yet.
</Note>

## The retention policy

The store holds a single retention policy, disabled by default. Sentinel ships the
mechanism without choosing a number for you: until you opt in, nothing is reaped. A policy
carries five fields:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `enabled` | Whether reaping and admission enforcement are active. |
| `horizon_days` | How many days of raw events are retained. Evidence past the horizon is reaped. |
| `reap_granularity_days` | The width of one reap bucket, and the storage partition width. |
| `backdating_admission_window_days` | How far into the past an appended event's occurrence time may sit. |
| `forward_admission_window_days` | How far into the future an appended event's occurrence time may sit. |

`RetentionPolicy::new` validates the numeric invariants at construction: the horizon must
be at least the reap granularity, and both admission windows must sit between the
granularity and the horizon. An event appended outside the admission window is refused,
and the refusal is recorded as a never-retained interval rather than left as a silent hole.

## Governance bounds

When a policy is enabled, its horizon must sit inside the platform governance bounds: **7
to 365 days**, inclusive. `RetentionPolicy::new` refuses an enabled policy outside that
range with `HorizonOutOfGovernanceBounds`. A disabled policy skips the governance check
because it makes no retention claim.

Read the effective tenant horizon with `RetentionPolicy::raw_event_retention_days`. It
returns the validated horizon when the policy is enabled, and `None` when disabled. The
helper never substitutes the platform default: `None` means no tenant horizon is
configured, and it is the caller's job to decide what that means.

## The envelope stamp

Every sealed verdict carries `raw_event_retention_days` on its assurance envelope, across
all five envelope families. The value records the horizon the verdict was evaluated under:

- When retention is enabled, the tenant-configured horizon.
- When retention is disabled, the platform default of **30 days**.

The disabled-path stamp records intent, not enforcement: it says no tenant override was
configured, not that a reaper is enforcing a 30-day horizon. Read the stamp back with
`Verdict::raw_event_retention_days()`.

`evaluate_published` in `sentinel-api` wires this end to end: it reads the store's
retention policy, maps a disabled policy to the platform default, and constructs the
evaluator with the resulting horizon. You only need the manual injection below when you
drive the core evaluator directly.

## Injecting a horizon into the evaluator

The core evaluator never reads the store or a clock. A caller that knows the tenant
horizon supplies it as validated data via `with_raw_event_retention`; without the call,
the evaluator stamps the platform default:

```rust
use sentinel_core::evaluator::Evaluator;
use sentinel_model::RawEventRetentionDays;

// Validated at construction: try_new refuses anything outside 7..=365.
let retention = RawEventRetentionDays::try_new(45)?;

let evaluator = Evaluator::new(specification).with_raw_event_retention(retention);
let sealed = sentinel_core::evaluate(evaluator, events, sources)?;

for binding in &sealed {
    assert_eq!(binding.verdict().raw_event_retention_days(), retention);
}
```

To configure the tenant horizon itself, replace the store's policy:

```rust
use sentinel_store::RetentionPolicy;

// Opt in: 45-day horizon, 1-day reap buckets, 7-day admission windows.
let policy = RetentionPolicy::new(true, 45, 1, 7, 7)?;
store.set_retention_policy(&policy).await?;
```

## Reaping and reaped evidence

`RetentionStore::apply_retention` reaps everything at or below the horizon and returns a
`ReapReport` naming what was dropped. Reaped ranges are tracked as intervals, so a later
read knows exactly which evidence no longer exists.

Missing evidence is never turned into a pass. A cold-path fact query whose window overlaps
a reaped range is answered `Refused(EvidenceReaped)`, which settles the binding as
authoritative `Inconclusive(epistemic)`. See
[cold-path guards](/sentinel/reference/cold-path#refusal-reasons) for the full refusal
semantics.
